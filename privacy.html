BabySleep — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

BabySleep helps parents log their baby's sleep and see when the next sleep is likely. We built it so that your family's data stays with your family.

We don't collect your data

BabySleep has no account, no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer cannot see your baby's name, birth date or sleep log.

Where your data is stored

  • On your iPhone and Apple Watch. The baby's profile and the sleep log are stored on the device and included in your device backup.
  • In your iCloud, only if you share the log. When you invite someone to the shared log, the log is stored in your private iCloud account and shared with the people you invite, using Apple's CloudKit. Anyone who has the invitation link can join; you can stop sharing at any time in Settings → Shared log. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server.

Purchases

Subscriptions to BabySleep Plus are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details.

Export and deletion

You can export your log as CSV or PDF in Settings → Export. You can delete everything in Settings → Delete all data, or by deleting the app. If you shared the log, people you invited keep their copy until you stop sharing.

Children

BabySleep is meant for parents and caregivers, not for children to use. It records information about a baby only as entered by the parent, and that information is not sent to us.

Medical information

Windows and tips are estimates based on age and your logs. They are not medical advice. If something worries you, ask your doctor.
