BabySleep

A baby sleep log that shows when to put your baby down, based on your baby's own rhythm.

Support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Write to svidina00@gmail.com. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I log a sleep? Tap Sleep when your baby falls asleep and Wake when they wake. You can change the times by the minute, and pause for short wake-ups.

How do I share the log with a partner or nanny? Settings → Shared log → Invite to the log, then send the link. Both phones need to be signed in to iCloud.

How do I restore BabySleep Plus on a new phone? Settings → BabySleep Plus → Restore.

How do I cancel the subscription? In the iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → BabySleep Plus.
